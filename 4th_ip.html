Research and Advisory

Ethiopia Coffee Farmers Annual Verification

2016 - 2025 • Oromia & SNNP

Since 2016, Insights Consults has partnered with Enveritas to onboard and survey over 50,000 coffee farmers across Ethiopia's primary coffee-growing regions. We successfully completed seven consecutive rounds of the annual verification survey.

Project Highlights

  • Verified sustainable coffee practices, land use, and supply chain traceability.
  • Executed precision polygon mapping and remote GPS traceability.
  • Supported EUDR-aligned suppliers (e.g., East African Holdings).
  • Delivered on time within a total project value of over $1.5 million.

Youth Health Campaigns (Voices & Level-Up)

Addis Ababa & Bahir Dar

Acting as the Monitoring, Evaluation, Research, and Learning (MERL) partner for UNICEF Ethiopia’s "Vaccinated Voices" and "Level-Up" campaigns alongside M&C Saatchi. The initiative applied human-centered design to amplify health and vaccine confidence.

Project Highlights

  • Reached over 2.9 million Ethiopians through live events and digital media.
  • Utilized participatory evaluation tools to address vaccine hesitancy directly.
  • Conducted diagnostic research, persona development, and message design.
  • Ignited essential health conversations in both urban and marginalized communities.

MFI Credit Data Integration

2019 - 2020 • Ethiopia Nationwide

As the technical support partner for the IFC / World Bank Group, Insights Consults successfully facilitated a massive data integration effort to improve the country's national credit reporting system.

Project Highlights

  • Securely integrated over 2.8 million borrower records from major MFIs.
  • Supplied vital data to the National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE) Credit Reference Bureau.
  • Provided comprehensive data aggregation, monitoring, and institutional coordination.
  • Contributed fundamentally to Ethiopia's 2019 Doing Business score for financial inclusion.

Conflict Sensitivity & Inclusion in Media

2024 • Ethiopia

Insights Consults led the endline evaluation of a prominent European Union-funded project implemented by BBC Media Action, aimed at fostering responsible and inclusive journalism across Ethiopia.

Project Highlights

  • Assessed current media trends to improve pluralism and ethical reporting standards.
  • Conducted extensive surveys, focus group discussions, and in-depth interviews.
  • Actively engaged working journalists, editors, and broad community members.
  • Presented actionable, evidence-based findings at a high-level national dissemination workshop.
